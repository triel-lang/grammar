# Contributing to TRIEL

Before a pull request can be merged, every author of its commits signs the [TRIEL Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) once. A bot asks for it on your first pull request; you sign by posting the comment it shows. Commit with an email address linked to your GitHub account, so that the bot can match your commits to your signature.

In short: the CLA lets the maintainer use your contribution in this repository and in any of the maintainer's other products, open or closed, with a matching patent license. A contribution to the specification files also receives the OWFa 1.0 commitment. You remain the author of your work. The text of [CLA.md](CLA.md) governs; this summary does not.

Which files are under which license is stated in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
